## Becoming a Maintainer

Consistent, high-quality contributions are the path to maintainership: code
reviews, issue triage, SDK parity work, or ownership of a platform SDK. The
governance model — including how maintainers are added and removed — is
documented at
[openiap.dev/docs/foundation/governance](https://openiap.dev/docs/foundation/governance).
Interested contributors can open a discussion or email <hyo@hyo.dev>.
