IAPKit is open-source purchase validation and entitlement infrastructure, managed by [OpenIAP](https://openiap.dev). It verifies App Store, Google Play, Meta Horizon, and Amazon Appstore purchases server-side and returns one normalized answer, so your app never has to trust local client state.

The hosted service is free under fair-use safeguards on shared community infrastructure. The source is MIT licensed, so you can self-host it instead.
